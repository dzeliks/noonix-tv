## What Noonix TV is

Noonix TV is a media player and browser for your living room, on computers, phones, tablets and
TVs. It comes with **no videos and no sources**. It plays:

- the video files on your own devices and network shares that you point it to, and
- whatever Stremio addons or Noonix TV extensions **you** choose to add.

Noonix TV doesn't host, index, upload or link to any videos. It doesn't check, approve or recommend
any addon or extension; the people who make and run them answer for what they offer.

## Your responsibility

- Only watch, stream or download videos you have the right to.
- The law differs from country to country. Following the law where you live is up to you.
- Torrents are off until you allow them. If you do, you download directly from other people, and
  they can see your IP address. Noonix TV only downloads and never uploads, but downloading videos
  you have no right to is illegal in many countries.
- On LG and Samsung TVs, torrents play through a small engine that runs on the TV itself. It also
  only downloads, keeps the video in the TV's memory and saves nothing.
- **Stream torrents to TVs** lets Noonix TV on your TVs play torrents through this device, while it
  is on and Noonix TV is open. It answers only devices on your local network, and each TV asks on
  this device before it can use it, so only turn it on at home on a network you trust, and only
  allow your own TVs. What it downloads for a TV is your responsibility as if you watched it here.
- Keys you enter (for a debrid service, TMDB, OpenSubtitles or Trakt) stay on your device. Noonix
  TV sends a key only to the service it belongs to, or to the addresses an extension you added lists
  for it, and it shows you those addresses before you add the extension.

Movie and show information comes from TMDB, watch history from Trakt, subtitles from
OpenSubtitles.com and the OpenSubtitles addon run by Stremio, and skip times from IntroDB. The speed
check downloads test data from Cloudflare, Hetzner or OVH, and the update check asks GitHub. Each of
them sees your IP address when Noonix TV talks to it. Addons use Stremio's addon format. Noonix TV
isn't affiliated with any of them or with Stremio, and using them is subject to their own terms.
